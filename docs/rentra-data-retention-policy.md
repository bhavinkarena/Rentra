# Rentra data retention and privacy fulfillment policy

**Policy ID:** `rentra-retention-v1`  
**Version / research date:** 1.0 / 28 September 2026  
**Status:** Provisional internal project baseline, created at the project owner's request for CP27. This is a development and operations policy, not a published customer notice or a certification of legal compliance. Automated enforcement is not yet delivered.  
**Accountable role:** Rentra business owner; the designated privacy lead operates this policy, finance validates accounting deadlines, and engineering implements and verifies disposal. Named contacts and legal entity details must be recorded before customer publication.  
**Next review:** 13 November 2026, then before the DPDP substantive commencement milestone in May 2027; quarterly thereafter and on material legal, processor or product changes.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Rentra keeps personal information for an identified service, evidence, security or legal purpose and disposes of it when that purpose and any applicable retention requirement end. Account closure must promptly stop new account activity and marketing. It does not authorize destruction of booking, payment, refund, settlement or dispute evidence still needed for an identified purpose.

This baseline covers customer accounts, owner/agent accounts, caretaker and admin access, booking operations, payments, support, disputes, reviews, uploaded documents, notifications, privacy requests, exports and backups. CP27's initial delivery scope is the **customer-account** fulfillment path. Other-role erasure, shared identity documents, processor disposal and historical-record disposal require their own reviewed actions; they must appear as outstanding or retained in the outcome.

The working jurisdiction assumption is India. Incorporation, GST registration, business locations, customer markets and processor contracts have not been established from the repository. Do not automatically apply Indian company or GST duties to an entity outside their scope. UK/EU obligations require a separate applicability assessment if Rentra targets or monitors people there. No payment-provider licensing or regulated KYC obligation is assumed merely because Rentra uses a gateway.

Periods below are **maximum operational targets unless a specific statutory minimum or documented hold requires longer**. Active service data stays while its purpose continues. A request to close an account brings the relevant review forward; it must not be postponed solely because a default period has not expired. All statutory deadlines are calculated for the particular record, not from the account-deletion date.

### Deadline calculation

Use the later **applicable** deadline for a record, not the longest period anywhere in this policy. For financial records, record the relevant financial year, its end date, any GST annual-return due-date extensions, the statutory basis and any proceedings disposal date. The interim accounting convention means FY 2026–27 records are first reviewed for disposal on 1 April 2035. A GST-only example with an annual-return due date of 31 December 2027 has a section-36 deadline of 31 December 2033. These examples do not presume Rentra's actual filing dates or registration.

The future DPDP one-year rule is an additional constraint for covered processing, not permission to retain every field for eight years. Before its commencement, implement a segregated, restricted retention path for required historical processing evidence. An erasure request must stop active use even when a historical copy must remain. Do not equate the 24-hour export download window with deletion of underlying statutory records; assess whether any separate record of the export processing is required.

## 4. Request handling and authority

1. Accept access, correction, withdrawal and closure requests through the existing account/privacy and support paths. A requester who cannot sign in needs a support-assisted verification path. Acknowledge within 3 working days; target an outcome within 30 calendar days of receipt. Verification delays or active obligations require an explanation and next review date, not an indefinite pause. These are Rentra service targets, not a claim that Indian law universally imposes a 30-day deadline.
2. Verify the requester using the existing verified account/contact, proportionate additional checks where risk warrants them, and documented authority for a representative. Never ask for a full Aadhaar copy merely to close an ordinary customer account. Store a verification reference and result; keep identity documents out of reasons and audit text.
3. Inventory this account's direct data, historical contacts, shared-person links, uploaded files, processors and backups. Check unsettled bookings, refunds, deposit/dispute cases and holds. Multiple roles sharing a phone or person record are not authority to export or delete each other's data.
4. A privacy-authorized admin records policy version, authority, requested scope, retained classes, reason and impact preview. High-impact commands require recent authentication, explicit confirmation and a version check. A reviewer may reject/defer an unsupported scope with a documented alternative and review date; they may not mislabel an acknowledgment as fulfillment.
5. At closure approval, block new activity, revoke sessions, withdraw marketing and revoke existing export access. Execute independently checkpointed disposal stages. A failure keeps the job recoverable and prevents a success claim for that stage. Never revive revoked sessions on retry.
6. Provide an outcome receipt distinguishing removed live fields, locally disabled methods, retained evidence/identifiers, completed processor actions, pending external work and failed/deferred stages. Account closure, partial anonymization and full erasure are distinct outcomes. Arrange a verified delivery method before sign-out; do not create an unprotected bearer receipt link to bypass a disabled account.
7. Maintain a grievance/escalation path through support and the designated privacy lead. Target acknowledgment within 3 working days and a substantive response within 30 calendar days; publish the applicable grievance timeline and business contact before relying on the public notice.

## 5. Access exports

An export is a scoped copy of authorized customer data, accompanied by an inventory and explicit exclusions. Include relevant profile, bookings, orders, payment/refund summaries, preferences, customer-visible support/dispute messages, reviews, privacy requests and file metadata. A file copy omitted for another participant's privacy must receive a reason and a route to a separately reviewed redacted copy; omissions must not be silently described as a complete copy of all data.

Exclude authentication secrets, OTPs, live sessions, payment tokens, credentials, internal-only case notes and other accounts' private information. Access rights remain assessed case by case; an internal label alone is not a legal reason to deny material the requester is entitled to receive.

Require live account/operator authorization at every download, encrypted storage and transport, no shared cache or public storage URL, expiry/revocation checks and an audited download. A UUID or a URL alone never grants access. Download receipts record metadata, not payload contents. Reissue through a new verified request after expiry; do not extend an old URL indefinitely.

## 6. Holds, disposal and processors

A hold must identify the relevant records, case/legal basis, accountable owner, start date, review date and release condition. Review at least every 90 days. A hold restricts disposal for those records; it does not authorize marketing, open-ended reuse or retention of unrelated profile data. Finance validates additional deadlines imposed by investigations/proceedings. Release the hold when its basis ends and recalculate disposal eligibility.

Run a monthly due-record review and a daily export-artifact purge. Until automated historical disposal exists, the privacy lead maintains the due-record and hold register in an access-controlled operations system and engineering carries out approved, auditable disposal batches. Do not store personal case registers in this repository. A missed disposal deadline is an incident requiring a documented correction plan; missing tooling is not a new retention basis.

Maintain a processor register covering hosting/PostgreSQL, Cloudinary media, payment gateways, messaging and any analytics. Record purpose, region, retention, subcontractors, deletion/revocation API, backup behavior and evidence of completion. Request processor deletion when appropriate; record an acknowledgement or failure. Provider transaction duties may differ from Rentra's duties. Retaining a payment token solely because a foreign key prevents deletion requires remediation, not a claim that retention is legally mandatory.

Disposal must cover production rows, object files/versions, cached/exported copies, search/analytics replicas and applicable processors. Validate record counts and referential/financial integrity. Keep a minimized disposal receipt. Do not casually overwrite immutable evidence to remove PII; first create a reviewed safe projection/archive strategy that preserves necessary evidence and restricts access to originals for the remaining justified period.

Minimum acceptance evidence: cross-account and cross-role denial; read/write capability separation; stale approval rejection; expiry and revocation denial; partial failure with no false success; restart without duplicate destructive effects; financial evidence unchanged; receipts listing actual removed and retained data; no raw identity documents or export payloads in audit logs.

Until those gates pass, customer messaging must say the request is received or under review. Do not display “all data deleted,” “fully anonymous,” “provider records erased,” or “DPDP compliant” based on this document alone.

## 8. Customer wording for later publication

The following is an editorial starting point for CP25 after implementation and contact details are validated, not a newly published policy:

> You can ask Rentra for access to your information, correction, withdrawal of optional consent, or account closure through your account or support. We verify requests to protect your information. When closure is approved, we stop account access and marketing and remove information that is no longer needed. Some booking, payment, refund, dispute and security records may remain for specific service, legal or security reasons. We explain what was removed, what remains, and any unfinished actions. Shared verification records and payment-provider records may need separate review. Recovery backups expire on their verified schedule; deleted information is restricted from ordinary use and must not be reactivated after a restore. Data downloads expire and require authorization. Contact and grievance details will be provided in the published notice.

## 9. Change control

The business owner adopts changes to this baseline; finance confirms statutory accounting scope and privacy/legal review confirms applicable jurisdictions and the DPDP transition. Engineering records the policy version used for each approved job. Preserve prior versions and case decisions; do not retroactively describe an old outcome using a new retention scope. Public notice changes go through CP25's draft/review/publication workflow and require matching operational behavior.
